03   SHOW YOUR WORK

Not just a
back-of-napkin
calculation.

We separate the beer from the glass, make our assumptions visible, and give you a plausible range—not a fake precision.

01
Beer mass

number of steins × volume per stein × fill fraction × beer density

12 × 1.00 L × 100% × 0.998 kg/L = 11.98 kg
02
Glass mass

number of steins × empty glass weight

12 × 1.30 kg = 15.60 kg
03
Total carried

beer mass + glass mass

11.98 kg + 15.60 kg = 27.58 kg

✳ This is the load in the glasses only. It excludes tray weight, movement, grip forces, and the very real challenge of keeping everything upright.
